Led by Prof. Yang (Veronica) Liu at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, we study how AI models can learn together when data must stay private. We develop federated learning methods for organizations that hold different parts of a dataset, and study how small models on personal devices can work with larger models in the cloud. Our applications span healthcare, science, and industry.
