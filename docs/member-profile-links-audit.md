# VERA Lab member profile-link audit

Audit date: 2026-10-07

## Scope and confidence rules

This audit checks only first-party or canonical scholarly sources: official personal and university pages, Google Scholar, GitHub, OpenReview, ORCID, DBLP, ACL Anthology, and arXiv. A name-only match is not accepted. Identity must also agree through affiliation, advisers, research topics, email domain, or an overlapping publication record.

- **High:** directly self-identified or linked by an official institutional/personal page, with matching affiliation or publications.
- **Medium:** canonical scholarly record with matching publications, but no direct institutional/self-authored identity statement or with a known indexing caveat.
- **Not confidently verified:** insufficient evidence to distinguish the person from namesakes.
